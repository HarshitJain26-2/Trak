Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 23, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Trak handles your information. By using Trak, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy. Trak is a developer-first project tracking and collaboration platform.

2. Information We Collect

Trak collects information you provide directly when creating an account and using the service. This includes:

  • Your name and email address (for account creation and authentication)
  • Project data, milestones, and task information you create
  • Team member and collaboration data
  • Device information for push notification delivery

3. How We Use Information

Your information is used to:

  • Operate the application and authenticate your account
  • Sync your projects, milestones, and team data in real-time
  • Deliver push notifications about project updates
  • Communicate important service updates

4. Data Storage

Project and account data is stored securely using Supabase, our backend infrastructure provider. Some data may also be cached locally on your device for offline access. We take reasonable measures to protect your data.

5. Third-Party Services

Trak relies on the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — for authentication, database storage, and real-time data synchronization
  • Google OAuth — for optional Google sign-in authentication
  • Expo Notifications — for delivering push notifications to your device
  • Firebase Hosting — for hosting this privacy policy page

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

6. Your Choices

You can:

  • Update your account information through the application settings
  • Manage notification preferences in the app settings
  • Delete your projects and associated data through the application
  • Contact us to request account deletion

7. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and secure authentication. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of Trak after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page when changes are made.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the project owner through the channels provided in the application.
