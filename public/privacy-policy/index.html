Trak is a developer-first project tracking and collaboration platform.

By creating an account or using the Service, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Service.

3. Information We Collect

We collect only the categories of information listed below. We do not collect advertising identifiers, precise location, or browsing history.

  • Account information: your full name, username, and email address provided at sign-up.
  • Authentication data: your password is verified by our authentication provider and is never stored in plain text by Trak. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google in accordance with your Google account settings.
  • Project and workspace data: projects, milestones, tasks, deadlines, statuses, and descriptions you create, as well as team memberships, project invitations, and join codes you generate or use.
  • Notification data: a device push token and basic device information required to deliver push notifications, only after you grant notification permission.
  • Session data stored on your device: your authentication session is cached locally on your device so you remain signed in.

We do not currently collect payment information, because the Service has no paid features.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we never have. We share information only with the service providers listed below, strictly to operate the Service, and only with other Trak users as described:

  • Supabase — provides authentication, our primary database, and real-time synchronization. Account and project data is stored in our Supabase project.
  • Google — only if you choose "Continue with Google"; Google authenticates you and shares your name and email with us. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles its own data.
  • Expo / device push services (FCM, APNs) — relay the push notifications you opted into. They receive your device push token.
  • Firebase Hosting — hosts this static policy page only; no Trak user data is stored there.
  • Other Trak users — your name, username, and project contributions are visible to members of projects you join.

We may also disclose information where required by law, regulation, or valid legal process, or where necessary to protect the safety of users or the integrity of the Service.

Where such transfers occur, we rely on the safeguards implemented by these providers under applicable law, including standard contractual clauses (SCCs), adequacy decisions, and equivalent transfer mechanisms. You can request further details about these safeguards by contacting us at the address in Section 2.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Active accounts: your account and project data are retained while your account is active.
  • Deleted projects: projects you delete are moved to a trash state and are permanently purged automatically after 15 days.
  • Account deletion: when you request account deletion via the contact email above, we delete your personal account data and your solely owned project data within 30 days of verifying your request.
  • Backups: residual copies may persist temporarily in encrypted backups but will be overwritten within the provider's backup cycle and are not used for any other purpose.

We may retain limited information for longer where required for legal compliance, dispute resolution, or security (for example, records of a deletion request itself).

8. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR and UK GDPR, you may have the right to:

  • Access — obtain a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete information (much of this is available in-app via your profile settings).
  • Erasure — request deletion of your personal information ("right to be forgotten").
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your information in certain circumstances.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent (for example, push notifications), you may withdraw it at any time via your device or app settings without affecting prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address in Section 2. We will respond within 30 days (or sooner where applicable law requires). We may ask you to verify your identity before fulfilling a request. If you are in the EEA or UK and believe we have not adequately addressed your concerns, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions where that is the minimum age, including parts of the EU/UK), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. 11. Security

We protect your information with concrete technical measures, including:

  • Encryption in transit: all communication between the app and our backend uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Encryption at rest: data stored with our infrastructure providers is protected by their encryption-at-rest controls.
  • Access control: database access is enforced through Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) policies, so each user can access only the projects they are authorized to see.
  • Secure account recovery: password resets use time-limited one-time codes, and passwords are never stored in plain text by Trak.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach, we will assess the risk promptly. Where a breach is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within the timeframes required by applicable law (including, where applicable, within 72 hours under the GDPR). Where a breach is likely to result in a high risk to you, we will also notify affected users without undue delay, describing in plain language the nature of the breach and the steps we are taking.
